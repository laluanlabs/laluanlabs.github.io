Jalan.

Jom, jalan! Navigation built for Malaysian roads.

JALAN is an Android navigation app made in Malaysia, for people who drive here. It is built on OpenStreetMap data and designed around Malaysian roads, junctions and languages.

The app is being built now and is planned for release on Google Play.

What JALAN is

JALAN is Bahasa Malaysia for “road”. It is a turn-by-turn navigation app for drivers in Malaysia. It shows where you are, finds a route and warns you about what is ahead.

It covers Malaysia only. That lets us focus on local details such as Malaysian road names, speed limits and the way people give directions.

  • Platform. Android first.
  • Coverage. Malaysia.
  • Languages. Bahasa Malaysia and English.
  • Map data. OpenStreetMap, an open map that anyone can improve.
  • Status. In development. Not yet on Google Play.

The bunga raya

The JALAN icon is a bunga raya, Malaysia’s national flower, with a heading arrow at its heart. The flower becomes the compass. Its navy, yellow and red come from the Jalur Gemilang.
