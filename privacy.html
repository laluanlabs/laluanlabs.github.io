Privacy policy

JALAN privacy policy

Effective 29 October 2026. This policy covers the JALAN Android app and this website.

Who we are

JALAN is developed by Laluan Labs, which is operated by Mining Resources, a business registered in Malaysia (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at miningresources00@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Location. The app uses your device’s precise location to show your position on the map, calculate routes and give directions. Location is used while the app is in use. Navigation can continue with the screen off once you start a route.
  • Reports you send. If you report a crash, hazard or road closure, we store the report, its type, its place and its time.
  • Basic technical data. We may collect crash logs and basic device information (such as Android version and app version) to find and fix bugs.
  • Messages to us. If you email us, we keep your message and email address so we can reply.

We do not ask for your name, identity card number, or payment details in the app.

How we use it

  • To show the map, search and route you.
  • To show driver reports to other nearby users.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.
  • To answer your questions.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with service providers that host or run the app for us, and when the law requires it. Driver reports are shown to other users without your name.

Storage and retention

Data is stored on servers we control or rent from hosting providers. We keep location and report data only as long as needed for the purposes above, then delete it or remove anything that could identify you.

Your choices

  • You can turn off location permission in your Android settings at any time. The map and navigation will not work without it.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you by emailing us.

Children

JALAN is made for drivers and is not aimed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy: miningresources00@gmail.com.
